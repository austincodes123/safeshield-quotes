Why NBIC Wins for This Address

This isn't just a price comparison — it's about which carrier is actually built to protect a home near the water.

⚓

Mariner Plus Endorsement

This is the big one. NBIC's Mariner Plus is a coastal-specific endorsement that broadens coverage for homes near water — addressing the exact risks that standard carriers exclude or underwrite poorly. Liberty Mutual simply doesn't offer this.

🏠

$200K More Liability Protection

NBIC gives Jonathan $500,000 in personal liability vs. Liberty Mutual's $300,000. For a coastal home where guests, contractors, and visitors are common, that extra $200K is real protection.

🔧

Ordinance or Law — Built In

If a storm damages this 1952-built home, local codes may require rebuilding to modern standards — that costs a lot more. NBIC includes 10% O&L coverage automatically. This matters enormously for an older coastal home.

💧

Mold & Fungi Coverage

Coastal homes face elevated moisture and mold risk. NBIC includes $10,000/$50,000 fungi, wet or dry rot coverage. Liberty Mutual's policy has an Amendatory Mold Exclusion — meaning mold claims may be denied.

🏨

Fixed Loss of Use Limit

NBIC gives a capped $100,000 for additional living expenses. Liberty Mutual's "Actual Loss Sustained" sounds generous but can create disputes during a claim. A defined limit means no surprises.

⚡

Equipment Breakdown Coverage

$100,000 for mechanical breakdown of home systems — HVAC, appliances, electrical. Liberty Mutual doesn't include this. For a 1952-built home, this is a meaningful add.
