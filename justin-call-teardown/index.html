02

Be the alpha. Confidence closes.

The method we are coaching to is Jordan Belfort's Straight Line selling (Way of the Wolf). If Justin has not read it yet, that is step one. Everything below maps to it: control the line, the three certainties, tonality, and looping to a yes.

1

Take command of the line

From hello to close, the producer drives the conversation down a straight line. The callback open, calling first and answering two questions, is exactly it. Bring that control to every first call.

2

Tonality is most of the message

How he says it outweighs what he says. Lower, slower, certain. The 64 "right?" tags and the upspeak on call one are the biggest authority-killers in the whole tape. This is the top priority.

3

Reposition, never just agree

Shift the mindset, do not validate the objection. The cash settlement reframe is the model: turn the cost into the reason to buy.

4

Build the ladder of yeses

Stack small agreements. Loop every objection back to a yes that raises certainty, then take one more step. A close is a string of yeses, not one big ask.

5

Sell Safeshield, not just Chubb

He sells the carrier and never sells the agency. Spend real time on why this client wins by having Safeshield steering it: the access, the advocacy at claim time, you in his corner.
