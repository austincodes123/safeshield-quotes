Bottom Line

Key takeaways

  • Same $1,687,000 dwelling plus an additional 50% (up to ≈$843,500 more, ≈$2,530,500 total), and same liability, med pay and water backup.
  • NBIC adds $100K equipment breakdown and folds the $2M umbrella into the $4,217 package.
  • Amica carries $421,750 more personal property.
  • Auto core limits match closely; Travelers runs about $901/yr less paid in full.
  • Full package: Amica $8,243 vs proposed ≈$7,499 — an estimated ≈$744/yr saving.
  • Home + umbrella runs ≈$157 more, offset by the auto savings and added coverage.
  • Confirm exact forms, deductibles and endorsements before any change.
