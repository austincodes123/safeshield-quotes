Coverage Breakdown

What your policy protects

These limits apply to both options. Tap any line to see what it means in plain English.

Dwelling (Coverage A)The house itself
$825,000+
Rebuilds your home from the ground up after a covered loss: structure, roof, attached fixtures, all at today's construction costs.
Real-life exampleA kitchen fire spreads and damages the framing and second floor. This coverage pays contractors to rebuild and restore the home.
Other Structures (Coverage B)Fences, sheds, detached garage
$82,500+
Covers structures on the property that aren't attached to the house.
Real-life exampleA windstorm drops a tree limb through the shed roof and flattens a section of fence. Both are repaired under this coverage.
Personal Property (Coverage C)Everything you own inside
$618,750+
Furniture, electronics, clothing, jewelry, tools, everything inside the home. With Replacement Cost Contents, items are replaced with brand-new equivalents at today's prices, regardless of age.
Real-life exampleA break-in and your safe, laptop, and TV are gone. Instead of a depreciated check for your 5-year-old electronics, you're paid what it costs to buy them new today.
Loss of Use (Coverage D)Somewhere to live during repairs
$206,250+
If a covered loss makes the home unlivable, this pays for hotel, rental, and the extra cost of living away from home while repairs happen.
Real-life exampleA burst pipe floods the first floor and repairs take three months. Your rental house, moving costs, and extra meals-out are covered.
Personal Liability (Coverage E)Protects your savings and future earnings
$800,000+
Protects you if someone is injured on your property or you're found legally responsible for damage to others, including your legal defense. Includes an extra $300,000 boost through the Advantage package.
Real-life exampleA delivery driver slips on your icy front steps, breaks a wrist, and sues. Your legal defense and any settlement up to $800,000 are covered.
Medical Payments (Coverage F)No lawsuit needed
$5,000+
Pays medical bills for guests injured at your home regardless of fault, a goodwill layer that often prevents small incidents from becoming lawsuits. Includes an extra $4,000 through the Advantage package.
Real-life exampleA friend's kid falls off the deck stairs and needs stitches and an X-ray. The bill is paid directly. No fault, no claim against you.
All-Peril DeductibleYour share of a claim
$5,000+
Your out-of-pocket per claim, and in today's insurance market, choosing $5,000 is a strategy, not a compromise. Carriers are raising rates fastest on policies with low deductibles and frequent small claims. A higher deductible earns you a meaningfully lower premium, and it keeps small claims off your record, which protects your rate at every future renewal. Insurance is for the big stuff; this structure prices it that way.
Why it's low-risk hereWith ProtectorPLUS, on qualifying claims The Hartford waives this deductible entirely, down to $0. You get the premium savings of a $5,000 deductible without fully carrying the exposure.
Real-life exampleHail damages your siding, a $22,000 qualifying claim. ProtectorPLUS waives the entire $5,000 deductible. You pay nothing.

The Detail Cheap Quotes Leave Out

Extended replacement cost protection

Most bargain quotes cap payouts at the policy limit, period. This policy is built with a safety margin above it, because after a storm, labor and materials in the Hudson Valley get expensive fast, and $825,000 on paper isn't the same as $825,000 in a post-disaster construction market.

Additional Limit: Total Loss to DwellingIf a total rebuild runs past your dwelling limit, extra coverage kicks in above the $825,000 so a construction-cost spike doesn't come out of your pocket.
Replacement Cost ContentsBelongings are replaced new-for-old at today's prices. No depreciation deducted for age or condition. A 6-year-old couch is replaced with a new couch.
Roof Value ScheduleYour roof's settlement terms are spelled out in a clear schedule up front, no ambiguity and no surprises at claim time.
Built-In Advantages

Why this policy punches above its price

◎ProtectorPLUS Zero Deductible
