One thing we want on the record about the Lexus. You asked us to quote it liability only, and we did. But we do not usually recommend holding liability only on a newer vehicle. If the Lexus is damaged in an accident you cause, or stolen, or hit by a falling limb, a liability only policy pays nothing toward the car itself. On a 2023 GX 460 that is a lot of value riding uninsured. Adding comprehensive and collision is a quick change and we are happy to price it, no pressure either way. It is your call, we just want it to be an informed one.
