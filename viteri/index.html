Our Take

Why We'd Go With NBIC

It's not the cheapest number on the page — but it's the best value for what you actually own here.

The short version

1

$663,000 in dwelling coverage vs. $500,000 from the other two — that's 33% more protection on the structure itself, which matters a lot on a rebuild after a total loss.

2

Replacement Cost, not ACV. If something happens to this 1800-built home, NBIC pays to rebuild it — American Modern pays you the depreciated value.

3

Cheapest liability and water backup premiums of the three, even while carrying full $500,000 premises liability.

4

Fair Rental Value / Loss of Use built in at $132,600 — if this rental becomes uninhabitable after a claim, that lost rent is covered. Neither competitor breaks this out.

Bottom line: for about $46 more than Foremost and $334 more than American Modern per year, you get a third more dwelling coverage, replacement cost settlement, and rental income protection. On a rental property, that's the coverage gap you don't want to find out about after a fire.
