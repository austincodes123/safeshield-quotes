20 vs 30 year

Why we recommend the 30-year term

Recommended · 30-year

Covered through your peak years

Thirty years carries you past the mortgage, through the kids growing up, and well into your earning years. The rate is locked the entire time. It costs a bit more per month, and it buys a decade more certainty.

20-year

The budget option

Twenty years is real coverage at a lower monthly, and it is a solid fallback if the budget needs to come down. The tradeoff is the coverage ends ten years sooner, when replacing it later will cost far more.
