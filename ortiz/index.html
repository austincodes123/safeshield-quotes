Coverage 01 · Home

The home, rebuilt to its full standard.

Everything starts here. Your home at 29 Rogues Ridge is written on Chubb's Deluxe form, which is designed to rebuild to the home's full replacement cost and to settle contents at replacement value rather than a depreciated number. These limits hold across every option below. The only thing that changes is the deductible.

Why we recommend the $25,000 option
The deductible waiver is the reason. On the $25,000 and $50,000 options, the deductible is set up to be forgiven on larger covered losses, so on the claims that matter most your real out of pocket is designed to be far lower than the number on the page. Moving from $25,000 to $50,000 saves only about $249 a year while doubling your exposure, so it rarely pays off. The $100,000 option drops the waiver entirely, which is why it sits separately.

Coverage 03 · Excess Liability

Protection that sits above everything.

Excess liability, often called an umbrella, sits on top of the liability on both of your homes and both vehicles, worldwide. Because you own two homes, five million is the sensible floor. It is the minimum that reflects what is at stake, and you can step up from there anytime.

What does an umbrella actually do? +
It sits on top of the liability already on your home and auto. When a covered claim runs past those underlying limits, the umbrella is designed to provide the next layer of protection, worldwide, including certain personal injury suits such as libel, slander, and defamation. Coverage 04 · Valuable Articles (PAF)

Your jewelry, scheduled the right way.

A Personal Articles Floater covers your jewelry on a blanket basis, worldwide, with no appraisal needed under Chubb's threshold and automatic coverage for newly acquired pieces. It is a small line on its own, but it does more work than its premium suggests.

Tap any line to see what it covers.
Jewelry, blanket +
$50,000
Covers your jewelry anywhere in the world on a blanket basis, with no appraisal needed under Chubb's threshold and broader protection than a standard homeowners sublimit.
Newly acquired items +
Included
New pieces you buy are picked up automatically for a period, so you are not exposed between buying an item and scheduling it.
Why we always add the PAF with Chubb
Writing the Personal Articles Floater alongside the home, auto, and excess rounds out the Chubb account. Chubb rewards a complete account with its portfolio discount, which is already applied across the home, auto, and excess lines on this proposal. In practice, the modest cost of the floater is designed to be offset by the account credit it helps unlock, so it tends to pay for itself while adding real worldwide coverage on your jewelry. Final discount eligibility is subject to Chubb's underwriting.

What Happens If

Three moments this plan was built for.

Insurance is abstract until it is not. Tap a tab to walk through how the recommended plan is designed to respond. These are illustrations, not promises of outcome.

Scenario · House Fire
A covered fire causes major damage to the home.
A kitchen fire spreads and damages a significant portion of 29 Rogues Ridge. The home is not livable for an extended period while it is repaired and rebuilt.
Which coverage layers may respond
  • Dwelling coverage is designed to address structural repair and rebuilding costs, subject to policy terms.
  • Extended replacement cost is designed to help if rebuilding runs higher than the $3,700,000 stated amount.
  • Contents at full replacement cost is designed to address furniture, electronics, and clothing at replacement value rather than a depreciated value.
  • Additional living expense at full coverage is designed to help with comparable temporary housing while repairs are underway.
  • On the recommended option, the $25,000 deductible is set up with a large loss waiver designed to be forgiven on larger covered losses.
Important assumptions
Assumes a covered peril, no applicable exclusion, and a claim reported and documented per policy requirements. Wind and hail losses carry a separate 3% deductible.

Scenario · Serious Car Accident
A serious accident generates a claim that may exceed the underlying auto limit.
A multi vehicle accident results in injury and property damage claims that, in total, may exceed the $500,000 underlying auto liability limit.
Which coverage layers may respond
  • Auto liability of $500,000 is designed to respond first, subject to policy terms.
  • If the covered claim runs past the underlying limit, the excess liability policy is designed to provide an additional layer of protection, up to the limit you select.
  • If an uninsured or underinsured driver is at fault, $500,000 of UM and UIM on the auto is designed to help address your own injuries.
  • Comprehensive and collision coverage is designed to address damage to your own vehicles, subject to the deductible.
Important assumptions
Assumes a covered accident, no applicable exclusion, and underlying coverage triggering before the excess layer. Actual outcome depends on liability facts, damages, and claim handling.

Scenario · Lawsuit Above Underlying Limits
A claim or lawsuit may exceed the home or auto liability limit.
A liability claim, whether from an auto accident, an incident at one of the homes, or another covered event, results in damages that may exceed the $500,000 underlying limit.
Which coverage layers may respond
  • The underlying home or auto liability coverage is designed to respond first, up to its $500,000 limit, subject to policy terms.
  • Once the underlying limit is reached, the excess liability policy is designed to provide an additional layer, up to the limit you select, for a higher total ceiling.
  • Excess liability is worldwide and is designed to respond to covered personal injury matters such as libel, slander, and defamation.
  • Defense costs may be addressed by the responding policy, subject to policy terms.
Important assumptions
Assumes a covered event, no applicable exclusion, and a claim within the scope of issued coverage. Final outcome depends on policy language, exclusions, and claim facts.
