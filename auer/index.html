Coverage adequacy

Making sure the numbers actually protect you

The cheapest quote is worthless if it under-insures your home. Here is why the coverage on this policy is built to actually rebuild 826 Rock Rimmon Rd, not just to look good on paper.

Dwelling set to rebuild cost

Coverage A is set at $1,608,000, higher than your current policy. This is meant to reflect what it costs to rebuild your 3,978 sq ft frame home today, not its market or resale value.

A 50% construction buffer

The additional 50% replacement cost option means if a total loss ever runs past your dwelling limit because material and labor costs spiked, there is a cushion built in above the number.

Contents at full replacement

Your $730,000 in personal property is written at replacement cost, so belongings are replaced new-for-old with no depreciation deducted for age.

Inflation guard built in

A 4% inflation guard nudges your limits up each year automatically, so your coverage keeps pace with rising rebuild costs instead of quietly falling behind.

We will confirm the replacement cost estimate together

Before we bind, we will walk through the full replacement cost estimate with you line by line so you are confident the $1,608,000 dwelling figure is right for your home. No guesswork, no surprises at claim time.
