Why it matters

The two things a restoration GL policy gets wrong

The pollution gap

A standard general liability policy excludes pollution. For a restoration contractor that means mold, sewage, and contaminated water, the exact work you do, would not be covered. Your package closes that gap on purpose.

The professional gap

You are not just laborers, you make calls on how to dry, treat, and remediate. If a client says you got that call wrong, professional liability responds where a plain GL policy stays silent.
