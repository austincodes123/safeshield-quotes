What's inside

Your coverage, card by card

The limits that matter, with a plain-English "what this actually means" on each. Tap to open.

If you cause an accident

The big one. Pays for other people's injuries and property damage when the truck is at fault, and pays the lawyers.
  • Per accident, injuries and damage combined$750,000
  • Legal defenseIncluded
  • Where you are coveredUp to 500 miles from Hamden
What this means in real life
You rear-end a car on I-91 with a vehicle on the hook. Two people are hurt and their car is totaled. Progressive handles the claim, hires the lawyers if it goes that way, and pays up to $750,000 for that accident. A tow truck loaded with someone else's car is heavy, which is why this limit is set higher than the state minimum.

The car on your hook

Called on-hook coverage. It pays for damage to a customer's vehicle while you are towing it, before their insurance gets involved.
  • Limit per loss$100,000
  • Your deductible$1,000
  • Who pays firstYou do, not the customer
What this means in real life
A strap slips and the customer's bumper gets torn off, or the car takes a hit while it is up on the bed. This pays to repair it, up to $100,000, and you cover the first $1,000. It is written direct primary, which means it responds even if nobody says you did anything wrong. That is what keeps a customer from calling their own insurance and pointing at you.

The cars in your lot

Called garagekeepers coverage. Once a car is off the hook and sitting in your care at 95 Burke St, this is what protects it.
  • Covered location95 Burke St, Hamden
  • Limit and deductibleAs scheduled on the policy
  • Who pays firstYou do, not the customer
What this means in real life
A car you are holding overnight gets sideswiped, vandalized, or stolen. This pays for it. Same direct primary setup as on-hook, so the customer never has to go through their own insurance. If you start holding more cars, or higher-value cars, we raise this limit. Just tell us.

Your own truck

2015 Ford F350, VIN ending 72583. Covers the truck itself if it is wrecked, stolen, or damaged.
  • Stated value of the truck$3,500
  • Deductible if you crash it$2,500
  • Deductible for theft, fire, weather$2,500
What this means in real life
The most the policy will pay for the truck is the stated value, minus your deductible. Right now that is $3,500. If the F350 is worth more than that to you, especially with the tow gear on it, tell us and we bump the number. It costs very little to raise and it is the one thing on this page worth a second look.

Hit by someone with no insurance

Uninsured and underinsured motorist coverage. Pays for your injuries when the other driver cannot.
  • Per accident$100,000
What this means in real life
Somebody runs a light and hits you, and it turns out they have no insurance or a bare-minimum policy. This steps in for your medical bills and lost income up to $100,000. In Connecticut this happens more than people think.

General liability

The part of the policy that has nothing to do with driving. It is also what most companies ask to see before they will hire you.
  • Each incident$500,000
  • Total for the year$1,000,000
  • Problems from a completed job$1,000,000
  • Damage to a space you rent$100,000
  • Small medical bills, no lawsuit needed$5,000
What this means in real life
A customer trips over a chain in your yard, or a car you dropped off rolls after you leave because a wheel chock was missed. This is the coverage that responds. It is also the line that motor clubs, police rotations, and property managers ask for on a certificate, so having it opens doors to work.
Put it to the test

What happens if…

Four things that actually happen to tow operators, and how this policy responds to each.

Loaded truck, wet road, you slide into the car ahead of you

Two people in that car go to the hospital. Their car is a loss. Their lawyer sends a letter a month later.
What responds
The liability part of your policy. Progressive assigns a claims adjuster, pays for the other car, the injuries, and the lawyers, up to $750,000 for the accident.
Your cost
The $2,500 deductible if you want your own truck repaired. The other side's damage has no deductible.

A customer's SUV comes off the bed with a crushed rear quarter panel

The customer is standing right there. They want to know who is paying for it.
What responds
On-hook coverage. You give them your policy number, Progressive pays the body shop up to $100,000, and the customer never touches their own insurance.
Your cost
The $1,000 deductible. Everything above that is on the policy.

You held a car overnight and it is gone in the morning

The owner is furious. It was in your lot, so it is your problem.
What responds
Garagekeepers coverage. It pays the owner for the car up to the scheduled limit, first, without anyone having to prove you did something wrong.
Your cost
The garagekeepers deductible on the policy. If you plan to hold more cars, we should raise this limit now, not after.

Someone blows a red light and T-bones the F350

You are hurt and out of work for six weeks. The other driver has no insurance.
What responds
Uninsured motorist coverage pays your medical bills and lost income up to $100,000. The truck is paid under your own physical damage coverage, up to its stated value.
Worth knowing
The truck's stated value is $3,500. If that would not replace your truck and gear, this is the scenario where you would feel it. Easy to raise.
