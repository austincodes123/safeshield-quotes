Coverage 01 · Home

The upgrade that matters: guaranteed rebuild cost.

Your current home policy pays up to a set number to rebuild, $2,208,000, and then it stops, no matter what rebuilding actually costs. The new policy works differently. It starts at $2,000,000 and it guarantees the full cost to rebuild your home, even if that runs past the number on the page. After a major storm, construction costs in Fairfield County don't ask what your policy limit is. This is the single strongest thing a home policy can do, and most carriers no longer offer it.

Tap any line to see what it covers.
Rebuilding your home +
$2,000,000 + Guaranteed
Pays to rebuild the house after a covered loss. Because it carries guaranteed replacement cost, the policy is designed to keep paying past $2,000,000 until your home is actually rebuilt. Your current policy stops at a fixed ceiling.

Furniture, electronics, clothing, everything inside, settled at replacement cost: what it costs to buy new today, not a depreciated number. This is raised from where you sit now.

Quiet extras built in +
Included

Why this is an upgrade, even though the number on the page is lower
Your current policy's $2,208,000 is a ceiling. If rebuilding costs $2.5 million, the difference comes out of your pocket. The new policy's $2,000,000 is a starting point, and the guarantee means the carrier keeps paying until the home is rebuilt. A slightly smaller number with no cap protects you better than a slightly bigger number with a hard stop.

Scenario · Major Storm Damage
A severe storm causes major damage and rebuilding costs run past the policy number.
A severe storm badly damages 5 Ledge Meadow Ln. After a surge in local construction demand, the rebuild estimate comes in at $2.4 million, above the dwelling figure on the page.
Which coverage layers may respond
  • Dwelling coverage is designed to address structural repair and rebuilding costs, subject to policy terms.
  • Guaranteed replacement cost is designed to keep paying past the $2,000,000 stated amount until the home is rebuilt. On your current policy, payment would stop at the $2,208,000 ceiling and the remaining cost would be yours.
  • Belongings at replacement cost are designed to be settled at what it costs to buy them new today.
  • Up to $600,000 is designed to help with comparable temporary housing while repairs are underway.
  • If the damage is from a hurricane, the 2% deductible applies, $40,000. Other covered storm losses carry the $10,000 standard deductible.
Important assumptions
Assumes a covered peril, no applicable exclusion, and a claim reported and documented per policy requirements. Flood is not covered by any homeowners policy and requires separate flood insurance.
Illustrative example only. Not a claim determination. Coverage depends on issued policy language, endorsements, exclusions, underwriting, and claim facts.
Scenario · Serious Car Accident
A serious accident generates claims that may exceed the auto limits.
A multi-vehicle accident results in injury and property damage claims that, in total, may exceed the $250,000 / $500,000 auto liability limits.
Which coverage layers may respond
  • Auto liability of $250,000 per person / $500,000 per accident is designed to respond first, subject to policy terms.
  • If the covered claim runs past the underlying limits, the $5,000,000 umbrella is designed to provide the next layer of protection.
  • If an uninsured or underinsured driver is at fault, $250,000 / $500,000 on the auto plus the umbrella's new $1,000,000 layer are designed to help address your own family's injuries.
  • Comprehensive and collision are designed to address damage to your own vehicle, subject to the $1,000 deductible, with valet claim service handling the repair.
Important assumptions
Assumes a covered accident, no applicable exclusion, and underlying coverage triggering before the umbrella layer. Actual outcome depends on liability facts, damages, and claim handling.
Illustrative example only. Not a claim determination. Coverage depends on issued policy language, endorsements, exclusions, underwriting, and claim facts.
Scenario · Lawsuit Above Underlying Limits
A claim or lawsuit may exceed the home or auto liability limit.
A liability claim, whether from an auto accident, an incident at the home, or another covered event, results in damages that may exceed the $500,000 underlying limit.
Which coverage layers may respond
  • The underlying home or auto liability coverage is designed to respond first, up to its limit, subject to policy terms.
  • Once the underlying limit is reached, the $5,000,000 umbrella is designed to provide the next layer, for a $5,500,000 total ceiling.
  • Defense costs may be addressed by the responding policy, subject to policy terms.
Important assumptions
Assumes a covered event, no applicable exclusion, and a claim within the scope of issued coverage. Final outcome depends on policy language, exclusions, and claim facts.
