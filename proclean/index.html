What's inside

Your coverage, card by card

The limits that matter, with a plain-English "what this actually means" on the ones worth understanding.

General Liability

The core protection when your work injures someone or damages property that isn't yours.
  • Each occurrence$1,000,000
  • General aggregate$2,000,000
  • Products & completed operations$2,000,000
  • Personal & advertising injury$1,000,000
  • Damage to premises rented to you$300,000
  • Medical payments$5,000
What this means in real life
A pedestrian slips on a wet walkway you just washed, or your ladder dents a client's gutter. This is the policy that pays their claim and your legal defense. The $2M completed-operations limit is the one that responds if a lighting install causes a problem months after you have left the site.

Property & Business Personal Property

Coverage for your location at 1698 Kings Ct and the business property you keep there.
  • Business personal property (RC)$10,000
  • Accounts receivable / valuable papers$25,000
  • Pollutant cleanup & removal$25,000
  • Property deductible$1,000
What this means in real life
Replacement-cost basis means a covered loss is paid at what it costs to replace the item today, not its depreciated value. Rated on your two operations: power washing ($150k sales) and exterior electrical/lighting work ($50k sales).

Tools & Equipment

Your gear is portable, so it needs coverage that travels with it, not just at the shop.
  • Contractors equipment$10,000
  • Equipment rental expense$5,000
  • Property off premises / in transit$25,000
  • Equipment deductible$250
What this means in real life
A pressure washer stolen from the van overnight, or a lighting reel that falls off a roof and breaks. This covers it, on the job site or in transit, with a low $250 deductible. If your equipment values grow, we raise this limit in minutes.

Crime

Protection against theft and fraud from inside the business as it grows.
  • Employee theft$25,000
  • Forgery or alteration$25,000
  • Money & securities (in / out)$25,000
  • Crime deductible$1,000
What this means in real life
Quiet but useful once you add seasonal help. If a helper pockets a customer's cash payment or forges a check, this responds. Small premium, real peace of mind as the crew expands.

Commercial Auto

Your work van, insured for liability and physical damage across both states.
  • Liability (combined single limit)$1,000,000
  • Uninsured / underinsured motorist$1,000,000
  • Comprehensive & collisionACV, $1,000 ded
  • Covered vehicle2014 Ford Econoline
What this means in real life
One $1M combined limit covers both injuries and property damage from an at-fault accident, and the $1M UM/UIM protects you if the other driver has little or no insurance. The van is PA-registered and this coverage follows it into New Jersey.

Put it to the test

What happens if…

Four situations that could actually happen on a ProClean job, and how the program responds.

Installing roofline lights, a ladder slips and cracks the client's window and siding

Third-party property damage from your operations, the everyday claim for a lighting and washing business working at height.
What responds
General Liability pays the repair and any injury claim, up to $1,000,000 per occurrence.
Your cost
No deductible on GL. You call it in, we handle the rest.

Your van rear-ends another driver on a New Jersey job run

You're PA-registered but working across the line. The coverage travels with the vehicle.
What responds
Commercial Auto liability covers the other party's injuries and vehicle up to $1M. Comp/collision repairs your van at ACV.
Your cost
$1,000 deductible on your own van's damage.

A serious injury claim settles for $1.6M

Rare, but it happens, and it's exactly what wipes out a growing business that only carried $1M.
What responds
Your $1M GL pays first, then the Umbrella adds its $1M on top: $2M of total protection standing between the claim and your business.
Room to grow
You can take the umbrella up to $5M as revenue climbs (see options below).
