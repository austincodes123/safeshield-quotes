A Few Things to Know Before You Decide

We believe in showing you the full picture — the good and the honest.

💡

The Home Rate is Higher — Here's Why

AAA stacked $405 in discounts for you as a new customer (membership, loyalty, new homeowner, zero claims). Plymouth Rock can't replicate that exact combination. The auto savings more than offset it at the package level — but it's worth knowing.

🏠

Roof Age — Exterior Inspection Likely

Your roof is approximately 21 years old. Plymouth Rock conducts exterior inspections on new policies and may flag roof condition. As long as the roof is in good shape with no missing or worn shingles, this should be fine — just good to know upfront.

🚗

CR-V Deductibles Are Higher on Plymouth Rock

Plymouth Rock's quote uses $1,000 comp and collision deductibles on the CR-V vs AAA's $500. That means $500 more out of pocket if you file a claim on that vehicle. We can discuss adjusting this if you'd prefer lower deductibles.

➕

Rental Reimbursement Not Included

AAA had $40/day rental on the CR-V. Plymouth Rock's quote doesn't include it — but it's easy to add and typically runs $40-50/yr. We recommend adding it back in.
