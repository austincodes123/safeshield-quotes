Why this structure works for you

Each building carries its own $350,000 limit, so a loss at one building is designed to respond on its own terms without depending on the other.

Buildings are valued on a functional replacement cost basis, a practical approach for properties of this vintage that focuses on restoring function after a loss.

This is surplus lines coverage, a market built specifically for properties that do not fit standard boxes, exactly like a four-unit, two-building, owner-occupied parcel.

Property policy fine print worth knowing
  • Roof coverage carries an actual cash value limitation and a cosmetic damage exclusion. With a roof around 12 years old, depreciation would apply to a roof loss.
  • Theft, flood, sewer or drain backup, and continuous seepage over 14 days are excluded.
  • The policy carries a 25% minimum earned premium. Terrorism coverage was offered at $292 and is not included unless you elect it.
  • As surplus lines coverage, this policy is not protected by the Connecticut Insurance Guaranty Association. The carrier group's A++ financial strength rating is a key reason we selected this market.
  • Occupancy matters: if the buildings fall below 31% occupancy for more than 60 days, certain coverages are reduced or removed. As an owner-occupant with tenants, this is unlikely to affect you, but it should be known.
Closing Day

Three things to do before you get the keys

This policy includes a few conditions that are easy to satisfy but important to take seriously. Handle these three items at closing and you start ownership on the right foot.

1

Working smoke detectors in every unit

The policy requires functioning smoke detectors in all units as a condition of fire coverage. Walk all four units on day one, test every detector, and replace batteries or units as needed. Keep dated photos.

2

Keep the heat at 60 degrees or above

Heat must be maintained at a minimum of 60 degrees in both buildings, or the plumbing must be drained and treated, for freeze related losses to be covered. Set thermostats and put it in your tenant leases.

3

Get signed leases with hold harmless wording

Signed lease agreements with hold harmless language in your favor protect you as the landlord and are information your carriers have asked about. We can point you to standard wording your attorney can review.

What this is for

A tenant's guest slips on an icy walkway. A railing gives way. A delivery driver is injured on the premises. These are the everyday exposures of owning a four-unit property, and this policy provides up to $1,000,000 per occurrence designed to respond to covered claims.

It covers you across both buildings and all rental units under a single policy.

Liability policy fine print worth knowing
  • Medical payments coverage is excluded. Assault and battery claims are sublimited to $25,000 with a $2,500 deductible.
  • Exclusions apply for certain water damage, animals, swimming pools, playground equipment, and construction work beyond routine non-structural maintenance. If you renovate, call us first.
  • This is surplus lines coverage with a $700 minimum due at binding and binding arbitration provisions. The quote is valid for 45 days.
