Why this structure works for you

Across the parcel that is $1,200,000 in building coverage, $100,000 for contents, and $50,000 designed to respond for lost rental income if a covered loss puts units out of service. When a building is being repaired, the rent stops but the mortgage does not. This is the piece that bridges that gap.

Special form coverage with theft included, the broadest property form in this package, written for an 1890 frame building in a strong protection class.

This is a surplus lines placement through a market built for properties that do not fit standard boxes, exactly like a four-unit, two-building, owner-occupied parcel.

Property fine print worth knowing
  • Buildings and contents are valued on an actual cash value basis, meaning depreciation applies to a loss settlement. With buildings of this age, this is how this market writes the risk, and the higher limits help offset it.
  • An 80% coinsurance requirement applies, which the $600,000 limits are set to support. Keep us informed of improvements so limits stay right.
  • Flood and certain pollution, virus, and cyber related losses are excluded.
  • As surplus lines coverage, this policy is not protected by the Connecticut Insurance Guaranty Association. The carrier's A+ XV financial strength rating through W.R. Berkley, a Fortune 500 group, is a key reason we selected this market.

What this is for

A tenant's guest slips on an icy walkway. A railing gives way. A delivery driver is injured on the premises. These are the everyday exposures of owning a four-unit property, and this coverage provides up to $1,000,000 per occurrence designed to respond to covered claims.

It also now includes a $5,000 medical expenses benefit, which can resolve small injuries quickly without a lawsuit, and a much lower $1,000 deductible than the prior option's $2,500.

Liability fine print worth knowing
  • Assault or battery claims are fully excluded under this policy, as are claims involving animals, swimming pools, and trampolines. Practical translation: no aggressive dog breeds on premises, and no pools or trampolines for tenants. Put it in the leases.
  • Coverage is limited to the designated premises at 18 South Main Street.
  • The liability premium is auditable: it is based on the exposure reported, and the carrier may adjust at the end of the term if the exposure was higher than reported. For a stable four-unit property this rarely moves.

Closing Day

Three things to do before you get the keys

Easy items, but they matter. Handle these three and you start ownership on the right foot.

1

Be inspection ready

The carrier will inspect the property within the first 45 days. Working smoke detectors in every unit, clear walkways, solid railings, and a tidy premises make this a non-event. We will give you a one-page prep list.

2

Get signed leases with the right wording

Signed lease agreements with hold harmless language in your favor, plus no-pets-no-pools-no-trampolines provisions that match the policy. We can point you to standard wording your attorney can review.

3

Sign and return the terrorism election

Federal law requires you to formally accept or decline terrorism coverage. Accepting adds $125 per year; declining costs nothing. One signature either way, and we will walk you through it at binding.
