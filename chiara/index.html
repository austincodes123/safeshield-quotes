Before You Get the Keys

Three things to do at closing.

The property policy includes conditions that keep coverage healthy. They're simple, but they're real, so we're putting them in front of you instead of leaving them in the fine print. Do these three things and check this box for good.

ITEM · 01
Working smoke detectors in every unit. All four.
The policy includes a protective safeguards condition requiring functioning smoke detectors in all units. Walk the property on day one, test every detector, replace batteries, photograph them. Fire coverage depends on this being true at the time of a loss.
ITEM · 02
Keep the heat at 60 degrees or above in both buildings.
In cold months, both buildings need heat maintained at 60 degrees minimum, or plumbing properly drained and winterized if a unit sits empty. This is what keeps freeze damage claims on solid ground. A smart thermostat in each building is cheap insurance on your insurance.
ITEM · 03
Keep the units occupied, and tell us if that changes.
The policy includes occupancy provisions: extended low occupancy can reduce or remove certain coverages. You're moving in and inheriting tenants, so you start in good shape. If a unit goes vacant for more than a few weeks, one call to us keeps everything aligned.
The House Rule
When something changes, call Austin before it becomes a problem.
