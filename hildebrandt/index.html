Why the Tiguan and Corolla swing so much

The 2011 Tiguan is carrying a $597 surcharge and the Corolla a smaller one on the NYCM side — those experience-related surcharges don't carry over 1:1 to Plymouth Rock's rating plan, which is a big chunk of the swing on those two cars. The Passport and Outlander price slightly higher with Plymouth Rock, so it's a net win across the household, not a win on every car.
