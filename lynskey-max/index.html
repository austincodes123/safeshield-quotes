Why lock the most in now

This is the cheapest it will ever be

Every birthday makes this more expensive, and there's no discount for waiting.

Term life is priced on age and health, and both only move one direction. The rate you qualify for today is locked the second the policy is issued, it can never go up for the full term. Wait a year and you're quoting an older, more expensive version of the exact same coverage. Buying the most you can now isn't overspending, it's freezing today's price for the next three decades.
