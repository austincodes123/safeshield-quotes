Coverage breakdown

What your policy protects

Tap any line to see, in plain English, what it does for you. These limits are built to fully rebuild your home at today's construction costs.

Dwelling (Coverage A)
The house itself
$1,127,500

Rebuilds your home from the ground up after a covered loss: structure, roof, attached fixtures, all at today's construction costs.

Real-life exampleA kitchen fire spreads and damages the framing and second floor. This coverage pays contractors to rebuild and restore the home.
Other Structures (Coverage B)
Detached garage, fences, sheds
$112,750

Covers structures on the property that aren't attached to the house.

Real-life exampleA windstorm drops a limb through the fence and shed roof. Both are repaired under this coverage.
Personal Property (Coverage C)
Everything you own inside
$789,250

Furniture, electronics, clothing, everything inside the home. Written on replacement cost, so items are replaced with brand-new equivalents at today's prices, no depreciation for age.

Real-life exampleA break-in takes your laptop and TV. Instead of a depreciated check for 5-year-old electronics, you're paid what it costs to buy them new today.
Loss of Use (Coverage D)
Somewhere to live during repairs
$338,250

If a covered loss makes the home unlivable, this pays for hotel, rental, and the extra cost of living away from home while repairs happen.

Real-life exampleA burst pipe floods the first floor and repairs take three months. Your rental, moving costs, and extra meals are covered.
Personal Liability (Coverage E)
Protects your savings and future earnings
$500,000

Protects you if someone is injured on your property or you're found legally responsible for damage to others, including your legal defense.

Real-life exampleA delivery driver slips on your icy steps, breaks a wrist, and sues. Your legal defense and any settlement up to $500,000 are covered.
Medical Payments (Coverage F)
No lawsuit needed
$5,000

Pays medical bills for guests injured at your home regardless of fault, a goodwill layer that often stops small incidents from becoming lawsuits.

Real-life exampleA friend's kid falls off the deck stairs and needs stitches. The bill is paid directly. No fault, no claim against you.
All-Peril Deductible
Your share of a claim
$5,000

Your out-of-pocket per claim. A higher deductible keeps your premium down and keeps small claims off your record, which protects your rate at every renewal. Want to see it at $2,500 or $1,000? Just say the word and I'll re-run it.

The detail cheap quotes leave out

Extended replacement cost, built in

Your coverage limits above carry an additional layer of rebuild protection on top of the base valuation. After a storm, labor and materials in the New York market get expensive fast, and a number on paper isn't the same as a number in a post-disaster construction market. This policy is built with that safety margin already in, so a construction-cost spike doesn't come out of your pocket.

Included in your premium

The extras already built in

Water backup coverage

Covers damage from backed-up sewers, drains, and sump overflow, one of the most common and most commonly excluded losses for homeowners.

Replacement cost contents

Belongings are replaced new-for-old at today's prices, with no depreciation haircut for age or condition.

Protective device credits

Your alarm system, updated heating, and newer roof all earned credits that are already baked into the rate you see.

Additional limits of liability

Extra dwelling and coverage limits kick in if a total loss costs more to rebuild than expected, so you're never left covering a gap.
