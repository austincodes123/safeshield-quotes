A note on your current home price

We shopped this hard and, honestly, we couldn't find a home quote anywhere near the price you're paying today. That usually isn't a pricing story. It's a coverage story.

The most likely reason is Replacement Cost (RC). Travelers is rebuilding your home at a $1,129,000 dwelling limit with an additional 25% replacement-cost cushion on top. If your current policy is priced well below this, the odds are strong that its dwelling / replacement-cost figure is set lower than what it would actually cost to rebuild your home today.

That's the "gap" we may not be seeing on paper. A lower price with a lower RC feels cheaper right up until you have a total loss and discover the policy won't fully rebuild the house. The number worth comparing isn't the premium, it's the dwelling limit behind it. Pull your current dec page and let's line up the Cov A figures side by side. That's the real apples-to-apples.
