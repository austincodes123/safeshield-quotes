Coverage Breakdown

What your policy protects

Tap any line to see what it means in plain English. Every limit below is included in the premium above.

Coverage A — Dwelling
The structure itself
$1,047,000

Rebuilds the home from the ground up after a covered loss: structure, roof, and attached fixtures at today's construction costs. This limit is boosted with an Additional 25% Amount of Insurance, so if a rebuild runs over, there's a cushion above the $1,047,000 before it comes out of your pocket.

Real-life exampleAn electrical fire spreads and damages the framing and second floor. This coverage pays contractors to rebuild.
Coverage B — Other Structures
Fences, sheds, detached garage
$104,700

Covers structures on the property that aren't attached to the house — set at 10% of your dwelling limit and included at no extra charge.

Real-life exampleA windstorm drops a limb through the detached garage roof and flattens a section of fence. Both are repaired under this coverage.
Coverage D & E — Fair Rental Value / ALE
Lost rent while the home is uninhabitable
$209,400

This is the piece that makes a dwelling fire policy work as a rental. If a covered loss makes the home unrentable, Fair Rental Value replaces the rent you'd have collected while it's being repaired, so a fire doesn't also mean months of lost income.

Real-life exampleA burst pipe floods the unit and repairs take three months. This coverage pays the rent that would have come in during that time.
Coverage L — Premises Liability
Protects you if someone's hurt on the property
$500,000

Protects you if a tenant or visitor is injured on the property and you're found legally responsible, including your legal defense costs.

Real-life exampleA visitor slips on an icy walkway, breaks a wrist, and sues. Your legal defense and any settlement up to $500,000 are covered.
Coverage M — Medical Payments
Guest medical bills, no lawsuit needed
$5,000

Pays medical bills for a guest injured on the property regardless of fault — a goodwill layer that often keeps a small incident from turning into a lawsuit.

Real-life exampleA visitor trips on the porch steps and needs stitches. The bill is paid directly, no fault or claim required.
Deductibles
Your share of a claim
$2,500 / 2%

$2,500 all-other-peril deductible for fire, water, theft, and wind, plus a 2% hurricane deductible that only applies to a named-storm loss. Both were priced as credits on this quote — a smart structure that keeps your premium down while reserving your out-of-pocket for the big stuff.

Also includedOrdinance or Law (10%) and Limited Fungi / Mold coverage — $50,000 liability and $10,000 property — are both built into the policy at no extra charge.
