Additional Protection

Coverage worth reviewing next

Professional Liability

Worth reviewing for allegations that the agency or a caregiver failed to properly perform promised services. The Scottsdale GL contains professional and healthcare-service exclusions.

Commercial Umbrella

An extra liability layer above eligible underlying policies. It may be requested by contracts and can help protect the business from a severe loss exceeding primary limits.

Employment Practices

Can address allegations such as discrimination, harassment, retaliation, or wrongful termination. Those claims are typically excluded by General Liability.
