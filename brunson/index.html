The three numbers, in plain English

Every pet insurance plan on earth comes down to these three dials. Here's what they mean on the plan you picked.

$500

Deductible

Your share before Fetch starts paying, once per policy year, not per visit. After covered bills add up to this, every claim until renewal skips this step.

70%

Payback rate

Once the deductible is behind you, Fetch pays back 70 cents of every covered dollar. You cover the other 30. Want 80% or 90%? Text Austin, we can re-price it.

$15,000

Yearly ceiling

The most Fetch will pay back in one policy year. Most years you never get close. It exists for the truly bad year, and that's exactly when the size matters.

The honest part

What it doesn't cover

You should never find out about the fine print during a claim. Here it is up front.

Pre-existing conditions

No pet insurer covers these, Fetch included. A pre-existing condition is anything Brunson has, or shows signs of, before coverage kicks in. Specifically:

  • Before you enroll with Fetch
  • Before the policy's effective date
  • During the waiting period of up to 15 days

This is the whole reason to enroll while Brunson is healthy. Anything that shows up after coverage starts isn't pre-existing.

Waiting periods

Accident coverage starts the day after you enroll. Illness coverage starts after a waiting period of up to 15 days. Anything that pops up before or during that window counts as pre-existing, so the clock is worth starting sooner rather than later.

Routine care (unless you add Wellness)

Check-ups, vaccines, spay and neuter aren't in the base plan. That's what the Fetch Wellness add-on above is for.

The policy's Terms and Conditions carry the complete list of exclusions and always govern. Details can vary by state.
