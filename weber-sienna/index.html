Homeowners · 123 Stafford St
HO-3 · Travelers Protect® · $1,944/yr
Quoted

This is the policy that protects the house itself and everything that comes with owning it. As a first-time buyer, the most important thing to understand is Coverage A, the dwelling, which is what it costs to rebuild your home from the ground up, not what you paid for it. Your policy is built to $339,000 with extra rebuild protection layered on top.

Dwelling (Coverage A) — $339,000Rebuilds the structure after a covered loss like fire, wind, or a burst pipe. Priced to today's construction costs.
Other Structures (Coverage B) — $115,000Your detached garage, fences, and sheds. Anything on the lot that isn't attached to the house.
Personal Property (Coverage C) — $125,500Everything you own inside. Furniture, clothes, electronics, all of it. And it's written at replacement cost, so you're paid what it costs to buy new, not a depreciated check.
Loss of Use (Coverage D) — $67,800If a covered loss makes the home unlivable, this pays for a hotel or rental and extra living costs while it's repaired.
Personal Liability (Coverage E) — $500,000If someone is hurt on your property or you're held responsible for damage, this covers you, including legal defense.
Medical Payments (Coverage F) — $5,000Pays a guest's medical bills regardless of fault, which often keeps a small accident from turning into a lawsuit.
All-Peril Deductible — $2,500Your share on a claim. Everything above it is on the carrier.
Included at no extra chargeWater Back-Up ($10,000), Personal Property Replacement Cost, and Additional Replacement Cost Protection (an extra 25% / $84,750 above Coverage A if a rebuild runs over). Those three are the details cheap quotes leave out, and they're already baked into your $1,944.
Real-life exampleA pipe bursts in the upstairs bathroom while you're at work and floods the first floor. The dwelling repairs, your damaged belongings replaced new, and a rental while the work is done are all covered, minus your $2,500 deductible.

Bodily Injury Liability — 250/500Up from 100/300. Pays for injuries you cause to others, up to $250K per person / $500K per accident. This is the layer the umbrella sits on top of.
Property Damage — $50,000Damage you cause to someone else's car or property.
Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist — 250/500Protects you if the other driver has no insurance or not enough. Matched to your new BI limits.
Comprehensive & Collision — $1,000 dedRepairs your own vehicles. Comp covers theft, glass, animal strikes, and weather. Collision covers crashes.
Rental + RoadsideA rental car while yours is in the shop, plus roadside assistance on both vehicles.
Why the 100/300 → 250/500 bump is smartYour current 100/300 premium is $2,606. For that, you more than double your per-person liability AND become eligible for the umbrella. That's one of the best-value moves in insurance.
Real-life exampleYou're at fault in a multi-car accident and two people are seriously hurt. At 100/300 you could blow through your limit fast and be personally on the hook for the rest. At 250/500 with the umbrella on top, you have $1.25M of protection standing between an accident and your savings.

Two things new homeowners always ask

“Why isn't Coverage A the price I paid?”

Your home's coverage is built on rebuild cost, not purchase price or market value. Land isn't at risk in a fire, the structure is. We set Coverage A to what it truly costs to rebuild, and added replacement-cost protection on top so a spike in labor and materials doesn't leave you short.

“Do I really need an umbrella?”

Once you own a home, you have real assets and future income worth protecting. A single serious accident can exceed your base limits. The umbrella is cheap insurance against the one claim that could otherwise follow you for years. With two vehicles and two young drivers, it's the smart call.

How to think about the cost

Two ways to look at it

Because you already have auto and renters with Travelers, there are two honest ways to read this proposal. The first is what actually changes for you. The second is the full picture now that a home is in the mix.

1 · What actually changes

You already carry the auto. The only change to it is raising your limits from 100/300 to 250/500, which costs about $174 a year more (roughly $145 pro-rated to your current renewal). That single bump is what lets us add the $1,000,000 umbrella for $629. So the incremental cost to level up your protection is small and targeted.
