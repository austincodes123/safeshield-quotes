Why This Matters

More Coverage For Less Isn't a Coincidence

Liability

$500K vs $300K

If someone gets hurt on your property or you're found liable off-premises, Homesite's $300,000 limit runs out fast once legal fees and medical bills stack up. Plymouth Rock's $500,000 limit gives you a much bigger buffer before your personal assets are ever exposed.

Personal Property

$426K vs $250K

Homesite's contents limit is nearly $176,000 short of Plymouth Rock's. In a total loss, that's the difference between replacing everything you own and paying out of pocket for the gap.

Why It's Cheaper

Discount Stacking

Plymouth Rock's $2,174 premium already has eight discounts baked in — New Business, Loss Free, Fire Protection, Security Protection, Agency, Deductible, Optional Coverage, and critically, the Auto Policy Discount for bundling. That last one alone is why the numbers work the way they do.

Real Protection

Water Backup Included

Sump pump and drain backups are one of the most common homeowner claims — and Plymouth Rock includes $10,000 of that coverage in this quote. It's not itemized on the Homesite dec page at all.

The Auto Side

Two Vehicles, Two Carriers — On Purpose

Here's the honest breakdown: Plymouth Rock will not write the 2015 Ford Transit. It's outside their eligibility guidelines for that vehicle class. That doesn't sink the bundle — it just means we split the two vehicles strategically instead of moving everything off Plymouth Rock.

The Play

The 2021 VW Tiguan stays on Plymouth Rock, right alongside the home policy. That keeps the multi-policy relationship alive and preserves the Auto Policy Discount worth $700/year on the home premium. The Ford Transit moves to Travelers as its own standalone auto policy. If we'd instead tried to place both vehicles together on a single non-Plymouth carrier, the home would lose that $700 bundling credit entirely — costing more than any savings we'd pick up on the van.
