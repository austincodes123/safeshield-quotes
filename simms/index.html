What the Extra Premium Buys

Not just a higher price — real protection

Now Covered

Wind & Hail

Completely excluded on the current property binder. A single storm loss to the roof or building envelope would be uncovered today. The package covers it, with its own wind deductible.

Now Covered

Theft & Vandalism

Both excluded today. For an occupied apartment building, theft and vandalism are among the most common property claims. The Special Form picks them up.

Now Covered

Sprinkler Leakage

Excluded on the current binder. A burst sprinkler or pipe event is a real habitational exposure — now covered under the package.

Now Included

Equipment Breakdown

Boiler and machinery / equipment breakdown coverage is built into the package — protection for heating, electrical, and mechanical systems the standalone binder didn't carry.

Before We Bind

A few things to know

  • The package is subject to a favorable inspection, a signed application & affidavit, a signed TRIA form, and 3–5 year clean loss runs (or a signed no-known-loss letter).
  • Carrier requires a written lease with commercial tenants maintaining $1M/$2M limits and naming you as Additional Insured. Worth confirming your leases meet this.
  • Both the current binders and the package carry a 25% minimum earned premium.
  • Like your current coverage, this is a non-admitted surplus lines placement — not backed by the NY State Insolvency Fund. Same status as today, no change there.
  • Terrorism (TRIA) is optional at +$565. You can accept or reject it in writing before binding.
