Employment Practices Liability Included

EPLI is included in your policy — this is a big deal. Most carriers charge extra for it.

If an employee or job applicant ever claims discrimination, wrongful termination, or sexual harassment, this coverage defends you and pays claims up to $25,000. Defense costs are built into the limit. The retroactive date matches your policy start: July 6, 2026.
