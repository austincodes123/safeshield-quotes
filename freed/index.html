Let's Talk About the Premium
Why the number looks the way it does.
A higher-than-expected number isn't always a sign the policy is wrong. Often it's a sign the policy is being built correctly.
Four reasons the number sits where it does
Reason 01
Prior Insurance Gap
Continuous insurance history matters more than people realize.
A 5+ year gap means Travelers prices this as a re-established profile rather than a continuously insured one. It's the largest single factor in today's number, and the one that rebuilds with time.
Single biggest factor
Rebuilds over time
Reason 02
Higher Liability Limits
The liability limits were raised on purpose.
250 / 500 isn't an upsell, it's the minimum underlying limit Travelers requires to attach the $3M umbrella. It's also the floor for a homeowner with future earnings to protect.
250 / 500
vs. 100 / 300 default
Required for umbrella
Reason 03
New Jersey Risk Environment
New Jersey changes the conversation.
Hudson County sits in one of the densest, most claim-active corridors in the country. A comparable policy in central NJ or PA would come in materially less expensive. This piece is structural.
NJ + Hudson County
Highest-rated corridor
Reason 04
Discounts Already Applied
Significant discounts are already built in.
Multi-policy, safe driver, anti-theft, EFT, and good payer discounts are already netted out. The number you see is the discounted floor, not a list price.
