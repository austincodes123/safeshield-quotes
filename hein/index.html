Why these limits fit Sam & Ayden

250/500 liability matches the equity they're protecting, a $732,000 home and two newer vehicles are real assets sitting behind that policy, and a lawsuit doesn't stop at a lower limit. $50,000 property damage covers the cost of today's vehicles, replacing another AWD SUV isn't cheap. UIM mirroring their own liability means if they're hit by an underinsured driver, they're not left covering their own medical bills and repairs out of pocket.

Why it's smart to run these limits bundled

Higher limits usually mean a bigger premium jump, but stacked with the Multi-Policy, Multi-Car, and Safe Driver discounts already on this account, the marginal cost of moving up from the state minimum is small. $1,000 comp and collision deductibles keep the monthly cost predictable while still meeting Travelers' preferred pricing tier, the sweet spot between a low premium and a deductible they can actually afford if something happens.
