What it tracks

Hard braking, fast acceleration, night driving, and how many miles you put on. That's it. No cameras, no audio. Just basic driving patterns.

After the first 6 months, Progressive locks in your Snapshot discount permanently based on how you drove. Good drivers save up to 30% more on top of what you already got.
