Justin's Take

Which one is right for you.

Honest answer: Option A is the right structure for this home.

Option B saves you about $99 a month. That's real money, and if the budget is tight that's a reasonable choice. But here's what you're giving up to get it:

Once the second floor is done and 62 Bryam Shore appraises at $4.65M, your equity becomes the largest target on your balance sheet. A serious accident on the Merritt or I-95 can produce a judgment well north of $1M. With Option B, your auto liability stops at $500K and your umbrella stops at $2M — total protection of $2.5M. With Option A, that number is $6M.

The difference between $2.5M and $6M of protection is $1,186 a year. That's the price of about one nice dinner a month for an extra $3.5M of asset protection sitting between your family and a courtroom.

If the savings matter to you, Option B is still a great Chubb package — better than what you have today by a wide margin. But if the question is "what would I do if it were my house" — Option A, every time.
