The Honest Take

Going with the $2,000 deductible saves you $17 over six months ($34 a year) but raises your out of pocket by $500 if you ever have a comp or collision claim.

That's a thin savings for a real jump in what you'd pay at claim time. On a paid off 2014 Sentra it can make sense to take the higher deductible, but if a surprise $2,000 repair bill would sting, the $1,500 option is worth the extra $17.
