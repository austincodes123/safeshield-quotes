One peril your home policy won't touch

Flood coverage, for about $50 a month

Private primary flood — 67 Valley Cir

Here's the part most homeowners miss: no home policy, however rich, covers flood. It's specifically excluded, and it takes a separate policy. The good news for you is that 67 Valley Cir sits in Flood Zone X, the low-to-moderate risk zone, so you're not in a high-risk area and no lender is requiring this. That's exactly why it's such a smart, cheap add.

Why buy it if it isn't required? Because being in Zone X doesn't mean no risk, it means lower risk, and it's exactly why the premium is only $602 a year. Over 40% of all flood claims come from outside high-risk zones. One backed-up storm drain, a heavy Nor'easter, or a burst from a nearby waterway, and a finished basement in a $1.7M home is a six-figure loss your homeowners policy flatly excludes. For roughly $1.65 a day, you close the single biggest coverage gap most homeowners never see coming. Since you're not in a high-risk zone, there's a standard 7-day waiting period, so locking it in now beats waiting for a storm in the forecast.
Two things worth pointing out

The coverage most quotes leave off

Extended Replacement Cost
+50%

An extra $847,500 to rebuild

Your dwelling is insured at $1,695,000, and this endorsement adds 50% more on top of that if a total rebuild runs over the limit. After a major loss, construction costs in Fairfield County spike fast. This is the buffer that means a labor and materials surge doesn't come out of your pocket. It's $173 a year and it's the difference between "covered" and "covered no matter what it actually costs to rebuild."

Blanket Jewelry Coverage
$0 ded

Your jewelry, covered as a whole

Standard home policies cap jewelry payouts at a low sub-limit and still apply your deductible. Blanket jewelry coverage raises the limit across all your pieces without having to itemize each one, covers them worldwide, adds perils like accidental loss (the ring slips off the dock), and pays with no deductible. On a $10,000 all-peril deductible policy, that matters, your jewelry is protected from dollar one.
