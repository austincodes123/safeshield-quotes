A couple things worth knowing

Why this coverage holds up

Guaranteed Replacement Cost

Your dwelling limit is $1,168,000, but the Guaranteed Replacement Cost endorsement means if a rebuild runs higher than that, the carrier still covers it. Construction costs spike after a big storm, and this keeps that gap off your shoulders.

Matched UM limits

Your uninsured motorist coverage matches your liability at 100/300 on both cars. If someone with no insurance hits you, you're protected at the same level you carry for others, which is exactly how it should be.

Full coverage on both cars

Both the Lexus and the Volvo carry comprehensive and collision, so theft, weather, and at-fault accidents are all covered. The $1,500 deductible keeps your premium efficient while still protecting the vehicles.

Pay-in-full saves you money

Paying the auto in full drops it to $968 and skips every installment fee. Same on the home. If cash flow allows, it's the cheapest way to carry the coverage.
