New to Commercial Insurance? Start Here

How a commercial auto policy actually works.

A personal car policy and a commercial trucking policy look similar on the surface, but they answer very different questions. Since this is your first one, here's the whole thing in plain English before we get to the numbers.

A commercial policy isn't one product - it's a stack of separate coverages bundled onto one bill. You choose the limit and deductible on each, and the total is just the sum of those choices.

Liability pays for harm you cause to other people - their injuries, their vehicles, their property. It never pays for your own van. This is the coverage the state, your customers, and the freight brokers you haul for all care about.

Physical damage (Comprehensive + Collision) pays to repair or replace your van after a covered loss, minus your deductible. Comprehensive is for theft, fire, weather, and hitting an animal; Collision is for crashes.

Motor Truck Cargo is the trucking-specific piece - it covers the freight riding in the back. Without it, a load that's damaged or stolen comes out of your pocket.

Your deductible is the amount you cover before Progressive steps in. Higher deductible, lower monthly cost - and vice versa. We set yours at $2,500 to keep the monthly reasonable while still protecting you from the losses that actually hurt.

Pays for injuries and property damage you cause to others in an at-fault accident - their medical bills, their vehicle, their cargo, and your legal defense. "Combined Single Limit" means the full $750,000 is available in one pool, however the claim splits between injuries and property. This is the number brokers and shippers look for on your certificate.

How the billing works

Commercial auto is set up as a down payment plus monthly installments over the term, paid automatically by EFT. On the recommended plan that's a first payment of about $4,528, then 10 monthly payments of about $2,264 - each installment includes an $18 EFT fee. Paying the year in full instead runs about $22,633 and skips the installment fees, if that's ever easier on cash flow. Either way, coverage starts the day your first payment clears.
