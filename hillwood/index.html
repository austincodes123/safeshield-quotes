Building & Property

Replacement cost on the structure the association owns
$500,000
›
Covers the physical building on a Special (all-risk), including Theft basis, valued at Replacement Cost with 80% coinsurance, plus Equipment Breakdown for the systems that keep the property running.
  • Windstorm & hail damage to the building — included
  • Equipment breakdown (boiler, HVAC, elevator controls) — included
  • Theft of association property — included
Real-life example: A winter storm tears shingles and soffit off the 70A roofline and drives water into the top-floor common hallway. The building repair and the equipment it damaged are covered — it doesn't land as a special assessment on the unit owners.

General Liability

If someone is hurt on association property
$1M / $2M

Protects the association when a third party is injured or their property is damaged in a common area.
  • Each Occurrence — $1,000,000
  • General Aggregate — $2,000,000
  • Personal & Advertising Injury — $1,000,000
  • Fire Damage (legal liability) — $100,000 · Medical Expense — $5,000
Real-life example: A guest slips on an icy walkway between the two buildings and breaks a wrist. Their medical bills and any resulting claim against the association are handled here — not out of the reserve fund.

Windstorm & Hail

The coverage your last policy was missing
Included
›
Named-storm and hail damage to the building is covered under this program — the single biggest exposure on the Norwalk shoreline, and the reason we moved the account.
  • Wind / hail deductible — 2% of building value, $2,500 minimum
  • Applies to the building; all-other-perils deductible stays at $1,000
Real-life example: A coastal system comes up the Sound and peels back roofing across both units. On the old policy that's a six-figure repair split across every owner. Here, it's a covered claim with a defined deductible.
