About the dwelling coverage difference.

Apples to Apples

Your NYCM home includes Extended Replacement Cost. That changes the math.

At a glance, Travelers shows $1.74M dwelling and NYCM shows $1.5M dwelling. That looks like a coverage gap. It's not. The NYCM policy includes the Additional Limits of Liability endorsement (NYC HO 500 25), which adds extra dwelling protection on top of Coverage A in the event of a total loss. With that endorsement stacked, the effective rebuild ceiling on NYCM is materially higher than the printed Coverage A number, and in most cases it lands above what the flat Travelers limit gives you.
