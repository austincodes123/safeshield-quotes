How it works

What level term insurance actually is

No jargon. Here is how these policies behave over their lifetime, in plain terms.

Level premium

The premium shown is locked for the full term. Todd's $371.11 a month holds for 30 years and Leah's $98.95 a month holds for 20 years. It cannot rise during that window regardless of age or health changes.

The death benefit

If either of you passes away during the term, the carrier pays the full face amount to your named beneficiaries, income tax free under current law. There is no medical repayment, no clawback, and no reduction for the premiums already paid.

Convertible

Both policies can be converted to a permanent policy later without a new medical exam, subject to the policy's conversion terms. If your health changes and you want lifelong coverage down the road, that door stays open without having to re-qualify.

Instant issue eligible

SwiftTerm can issue without a paramedical exam when the carrier's data checks clear. For two healthy applicants that often means coverage in force in days rather than the usual six to eight weeks. It is not guaranteed and the carrier makes the final call.

When the term ends

At the end of the term the coverage simply expires. There is no payout at the end and no cash value to collect. Term insurance is pure protection during the years your family depends on your income, which is what makes it inexpensive relative to the benefit.

Beneficiaries

You name who receives the benefit, and you can name primary and contingent beneficiaries. With minor children it is worth a short conversation about whether a trust should be the beneficiary rather than the children directly. We will cover that before anything is finalized.

On the different term lengths. Todd's is a 30 year term and Leah's is a 20 year term, which is exactly what you selected. They do not need to match. Each policy stands on its own, is priced on its own, and can be changed or cancelled independently at any time with no penalty.
What happens next

From decided to in force

You have made the decision. Everything from here is process, and I carry most of it.

Applications

Electronic, roughly twenty minutes each, done from home. One for Todd, one for Leah. I can be on the phone with you for the first one if you want.

Underwriting

Because both are SwiftTerm, the carrier runs its instant-issue data checks first. If everything clears there may be no exam at all. If a paramedical visit is needed, the examiner comes to the house at a time you pick.

Carrier offer

Symetra returns the final health class. If it matches the preferred non-tobacco pricing shown here, nothing changes. If it comes back different, you hear it from me before anyone signs, and we look at options.

Beneficiaries and payment

We set primary and contingent beneficiaries, decide on payment frequency, and set up the first premium. This is also where we settle the trust question if it applies.

Policy delivered, coverage in force

The carrier issues the policy, it is delivered to you, and the coverage is active. You get the documents for your records and we are done.
