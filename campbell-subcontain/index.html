👥
Workers' Compensation & Employer's Liability
Required by the franchise agreement. You mentioned you don't have employees right now and aren't sure you need this — that's a completely fair question. Here's the important thing: in Connecticut, if you are a sole owner/operator of an LLC, you may be able to exclude yourself from Workers' Comp coverage. BUT — your franchise agreement requires it, which means you likely need it regardless. Before we put anything through, please confirm with your franchisor whether a sole-owner exemption is acceptable under your agreement, or whether they require a policy in place regardless. We don't want you to pay for something unnecessarily — but we also don't want you to be out of compliance on day one.
Confirm with Franchisor Before Placing
👤
Employment Practices Liability (EPLI)
Required by the franchise agreement. You mentioned you don't have employees yet and questioned whether this is necessary. Valid concern — EPLI is designed for wrongful termination, harassment, and discrimination claims from employees. However, your franchise agreement lists it as required, and some carriers will still require it even for sole operators because of contractor or subcontractor relationships. Same recommendation: confirm with your franchisor whether this is required before you bring on any staff, or if they need it from day one.
Confirm with Franchisor Before Placing
🔒
Commercial Crime / Employee Dishonesty
Required by the franchise agreement. Similar to the others — this covers theft or fraud committed by employees. As a solo operator, you may not need this immediately. But again, the franchise agreement requires it. Confirm with your franchisor whether this needs to be in place on Day 1, even without employees. We'll get it quoted so it's ready to go when the time comes.
Confirm with Franchisor Before Placing
