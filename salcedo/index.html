The gap this closes

$25,000 per person / $50,000 per accident is the legal minimum in New York. It sounds like coverage, but it's really just enough to get you sued. If Mario or Cassandra were ever in a serious accident and someone was seriously hurt, medical bills and lost wages blow past $50,000 almost immediately — hospital stays alone can run six figures.

When that happens, the injured party's attorney doesn't stop at your policy limit. They come after you personally — your bank accounts, your investments, and yes, your new home at 94 Hess Rd. That's the exposure a home creates that didn't exist when you were renting.

We took your liability to 250/500 — exactly 10x your old limits — and then stacked a $1,000,000 umbrella on top of both your auto and home policies. The umbrella doesn't kick in until your underlying auto/home liability is exhausted, which is exactly why we needed those underlying limits raised first. Together, they mean a bad day on the road can never turn into losing the house you just bought.
