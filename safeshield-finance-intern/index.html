Position Summary

We're looking for a sharp, motivated Finance Intern to join our growing Financial Planning and Wealth Division based in Stamford, CT. You'll work directly alongside senior leadership — not just in financial analysis and reporting, but in active M&A deal flow as Safeshield continues to acquire and integrate independent agencies across the Northeast. This is hands-on work with real impact.

M&A Division — Direct Exposure from Day One

Safeshield is actively pursuing agency acquisitions across the Northeast. As a Finance Intern, you'll be embedded in that process — not watching from the sidelines.

  • Support financial due diligence on target agency acquisitions — reviewing books of business, premium volume, retention rates, and carrier mix
  • Assist in building acquisition models, valuation analyses, and LOI support packages
  • Work directly with leadership to evaluate agency financials and identify integration opportunities
  • Sit in on deal calls and strategy sessions with ownership
  • Help build the M&A pipeline infrastructure — tracking targets, maintaining deal flow databases, and preparing executive summaries
Key Responsibilities
  • Financial Analysis: Analyze financial data, prepare reports, and develop insights to support leadership decision-making
  • Budgeting & Forecasting: Help prepare and review budgets, forecasts, and financial projections across divisions
  • Data Management: Collect, organize, and maintain financial data across spreadsheets, CRMs, and deal-tracking systems
  • Market Research: Conduct research on industry trends, acquisition targets, and financial products to support active projects
  • Reporting: Assist in preparing monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports and executive presentations
  • Client & Wealth Support: Support the financial planning team in building tailored plans for high-net-worth clients
  • Compliance: Ensure adherence to company policies, procedures, and regulatory requirements
  • Ad-Hoc Projects: Contribute to special initiatives as needed — no two days look the same here

Required Qualifications

  • Pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field
  • Strong analytical skills and attention to detail
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Office Suite
  • Basic understanding of financial statements and accounting principles
  • Excellent verbal and written communication skills
  • Ability to work independently and take ownership

Preferred Qualifications

  • Prior internship or work experience in finance, banking, or M&A
  • Knowledge of financial modeling, SQL, Tableau, or Python
  • Familiarity with insurance industry financials or agency operations
  • Experience with CRM tools or deal-tracking software
  • Entrepreneurial mindset — you see problems and solve them
