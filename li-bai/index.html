Home + Auto Insurance Comparison

Two strong ways to protect your home.

One option delivers Chubb's high-limit private-client protection. The other uses NBIC and Plymouth Rock to maintain strong core coverage at a substantially lower annual cost.

62 Byram Shore Rd, Greenwich, CT 06830

The decision is protection versus price.

Chubb carries much higher home, contents, and specialty limits. NBIC plus Plymouth Rock saves roughly $8,000 per year and gives you lower deductibles. The right fit depends on how much risk you want the policy to absorb.

The Plymouth Rock Package

NBIC home and Plymouth Rock auto work together

Narragansett Bay Insurance Company is the homeowners side of this Plymouth Rock package. The home and auto are written on separate policies, but they are presented together as one coordinated alternative to Chubb.

NBIC Homeowners

$9,322 per year for HO-5 coverage with a $2,582,000 dwelling limit, 50% additional dwelling protection, $750,000 of replacement-cost personal property, $500,000 liability, and a $10,000 all-peril deductible.

Plymouth Rock Auto

$2,002 paid in full or $2,113 on an installment plan for the two Mercedes vehicles, with $500,000 bodily injury and UM/UIM limits and $2,000 comprehensive and collision deductibles.

Combined Package

$11,324 annually when paid in full, or $11,435 using the Plymouth Rock auto installment premium. This is the complete NBIC plus Plymouth Rock alternative shown throughout the comparison.
