What is Snapshot?

Snapshot is a free app-based program from Progressive that tracks your driving habits — things like speed, braking, and time of day. Safe drivers typically save money. You start with a participation discount just for enrolling, and your final rate is adjusted based on how you actually drive. It never makes your rate go up — only down.
