Your coverage

What your policy protects

These are the six core coverages on your policy. Tap any line to see what it actually does for you.

Dwelling · Coverage A
The house itself
$509,000

Rebuilds your home from the ground up after a covered loss: the structure, roof, and everything attached, priced at today's construction costs.

Real-life exampleA kitchen fire spreads and damages the framing. This coverage pays contractors to rebuild and restore the home.
Other Structures · Coverage B
Detached garage, fences, sheds
$50,900

Covers the structures on your property that aren't attached to the house, like your detached garage.

Real-life exampleA windstorm drops a tree limb through the garage roof and flattens a section of fence. Both are repaired under this coverage.
Personal Property · Coverage C
Everything you own inside
$254,500

Furniture, electronics, clothing, everything inside the home. And because you have Replacement Cost settlement (see endorsements below), items are replaced new-for-old, not depreciated for age.

Real-life exampleA break-in takes your laptop and TV. Instead of a depreciated check for 5-year-old electronics, you're paid what it costs to buy them new today.
Loss of Use · Coverage D
Somewhere to live during repairs
$101,800

If a covered loss makes the home unlivable, this pays for a hotel or rental and the extra cost of living away from home while repairs happen. Worth noting: Travelers flagged this limit as lower than what they commonly provide. It's easy to bump up if you'd like, just say the word.

Real-life exampleA burst pipe floods the first floor and repairs take three months. Your rental, moving costs, and extra meals out are covered.
Personal Liability · Coverage E
Protects your savings & future earnings
$500,000

Protects you if someone is injured on your property or you're found legally responsible for damage to others, including your legal defense costs.

Real-life exampleA delivery driver slips on your front steps, breaks a wrist, and sues. Your legal defense and any settlement up to $500,000 are covered.
Medical Payments · Coverage F
No lawsuit needed
$1,000

Pays medical bills for guests injured at your home regardless of fault, a goodwill layer that often keeps small incidents from turning into lawsuits.

Real-life exampleA friend trips on the deck stairs and needs stitches. The bill is paid directly, no fault, no claim against you.
All-Peril Deductible
Your share of a claim
$2,500

This is what you pay out of pocket on a covered claim before the policy kicks in. $2,500 is a balanced spot: low enough to be manageable, high enough to keep your premium reasonable and small claims off your record.

Real-life exampleHail damages your siding, a $22,000 repair. You pay your $2,500 deductible and Travelers covers the remaining $19,500.
Endorsements included

The extras that make this policy stronger

These are add-ons layered onto your base policy. Two are already baked into your premium at no extra cost, and one adds valuable protection for just $16 a year.

Included

Water Backup & Sump Overflow

Adds $10,000 of coverage for damage from backed-up sewers, drains, and sump pump overflow, one of the most common losses a standard policy leaves out. Especially valuable with a basement foundation.

Included

Personal Property Replacement Cost

Your belongings are replaced with brand-new equivalents at today's prices, with no depreciation deducted for age or wear. A 6-year-old couch is replaced with a new couch.

+$16/yr

Personal Injury Coverage

Extends your liability protection beyond bodily injury to cover things like libel, slander, defamation, and wrongful eviction. A small premium for meaningful modern protection.
