The idea in one line

Same coverage. Its own bucket.

Your company policy protects every film you distribute out of one shared limit. This policy gives MIROIRS its own dedicated limit, so a claim on this one film can't drain the protection your other titles rely on, and nothing else on your slate can drain MIROIRS.

It isn't extra protection. It's separation.

The coverage, the carrier, and the terms are the same you already have. What changes is where a claim gets paid from.

Think of your annual limit as a bucket of water. Under the company policy, every film drinks from the same bucket. This policy hands MIROIRS a bucket of its own.

Worth knowing, plainly

The fine print, without the fog.

The two policies don't stack

If a single claim ever touched both this policy and your company policy, you can't collect both limits. You're capped at the higher single limit. That's normal anti-double-dip language, and it doesn't reduce the ring-fence, since MIROIRS is carved out of the company policy anyway.

Surplus lines, strong carrier

This is placed with Lloyd's of London, one of the most established markets in the world, rated A+ Superior. Because it's non-admitted, it isn't backed by the New York state guaranty fund, which is standard for specialty film coverage.

Occurrence form

The policy responds to events that happen during the policy period, which suits a distribution window well. We'll keep an eye on the term against your license dates.

One deductible per claim

You cover the first $10,000 of each claim. The policy pays above that, up to $1M per claim and $3M for the year across all claims combined.
