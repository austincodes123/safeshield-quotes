What You're Covered For
Your Coverage, Explained Simply

Here's exactly what the recommended Travelers policy covers — in plain English.

🚗
Bodily Injury Liability
$100,000 / $300,000
If you cause an accident and injure someone, this pays their medical bills and lost wages — up to $100K per person, $300K per accident. It also pays your legal defense if you get sued.
🏘️
Property Damage
$100,000
Covers damage you cause to someone else's car, fence, building, or property. $100K is a strong limit — the average car accident causes around $4,700 in property damage.
🛡️
Uninsured / Underinsured Motorist
$100,000 / $300,000
Protects YOU if someone hits you and they have no insurance or not enough. About 12% of CT drivers are uninsured — this coverage is essential.
🏥
Medical Payments
$5,000 per person
Pays medical bills for you and your passengers regardless of fault. No deductible — it kicks in immediately after an accident.
🌪️
Comprehensive
$1,000 deductible
Covers theft, fire, flood, hail, hitting a deer, vandalism — anything that isn't a collision. Also includes $0 glass deductible for chip/crack repairs.
💥
Collision
$1,000 deductible
Covers repairs to your car if you hit another vehicle or object — regardless of fault. Your deductible is what you pay first; Travelers covers the rest.
🔑
Loan/Lease Gap
Included
If your vehicle is totaled and you owe more than it's worth, this pays the difference between the insurance payout and your loan balance. Travelers includes this on BOTH vehicles — a real differentiator.
🚨
Roadside Assistance
$15/vehicle
Towing, jump starts, flat tire help, lockout service. Peace of mind for under $30/year across the whole policy.
