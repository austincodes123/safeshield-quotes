Independent Contractor Agreement

State of Connecticut

This Independent Contractor Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of __________________, 2026, by and between Safeshield Insurance LLC, a Connecticut limited liability company with a principal place of business at 2777 Summer Street, Stamford, CT 06905 ("Safeshield" or the "Company"), and Trent Beggs, residing at ______________________________ ("Contractor"). Safeshield and Contractor may each be referred to as a "Party" and together as the "Parties."

1. Scope of Services

Contractor shall provide insurance-related services to Safeshield, which may include lead handling, outbound calling, quoting, client communications, servicing support, sales activity, and other duties assigned by Safeshield from time to time (the "Services"). Safeshield may modify, expand, reduce, or change the Services, expectations, focus areas, performance standards, or workflows at its discretion.

Contractor acknowledges that unless properly licensed, Contractor shall not quote, bind, advise on coverage, or hold themselves out as a licensed insurance producer.

2. Licensing and Regulatory Compliance

Contractor shall at all times maintain any licenses required to perform the Services and shall comply with all applicable state and federal insurance laws, carrier guidelines, and Company policies. Any lapse, suspension, or revocation of a required license must be reported to Safeshield immediately and may result in suspension of compensation or termination of this Agreement.

3. Compensation

Contractor shall be paid a percentage of the commission Safeshield earns on policies written by Contractor, based on the source of the business and Contractor's engagement status, as set forth below. Commission is paid in accordance with the Company's standard commission schedule and is subject to receipt of funds from the applicable carrier.

StatusSafeshield-generatedContractor-generatedBase
Full-Time20%35%$750 / month
Part-Time (enrolled in college / not full-time)30%40%None

"Safeshield-generated" means accounts sourced through Company leads, the dealer pipeline, inbound, referrals, or other Company channels. "Contractor-generated" means accounts Contractor personally originates. The applicable split applies to both new business and renewals on accounts written by Contractor for so long as this Agreement remains in effect.

The compensation terms above are reviewed together by the Parties on an annual basis and may be adjusted by mutual agreement. Compensation is otherwise discretionary and may be changed by the Company on a prospective basis. Any bonuses or production incentives are at the Company's sole discretion.

4. Expenses

Contractor is responsible for Contractor's own ordinary business expenses unless Safeshield agrees in writing to reimburse a specific expense in advance.

5. Term and Termination

This Agreement begins on the effective date and continues until terminated. Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, upon written notice. Upon termination, Contractor is entitled only to compensation earned through the effective date of termination, subject to the other terms of this Agreement.

6. Licensing Costs and Repayment

The Company has paid, or will pay, certain costs to obtain and maintain Contractor's insurance license and related onboarding, which may include pre-licensing education, examination fees, application and state licensing fees, fingerprinting, and appointment costs (collectively, the "Licensing Costs"), in the total documented amount of approximately $1,900. These costs are advanced for Contractor's benefit.

If Contractor terminates this Agreement, or the Company terminates this Agreement for cause, within twenty-four (24) months of the effective date, Contractor shall reimburse the Company for the unforgiven portion of the Licensing Costs. The Licensing Costs are forgiven in equal monthly increments over that twenty-four (24) month period, such that one twenty-fourth (1/24) is forgiven for each full month Contractor remains engaged, and any remaining unforgiven balance becomes due upon departure. No amount is owed if the Company terminates this Agreement without cause, or if Contractor remains engaged beyond the twenty-four (24) month period.

Contractor authorizes the Company to offset and deduct any amount owed under this Section from any final compensation, commissions, or other amounts otherwise payable to Contractor, with any remaining balance due within thirty (30) days of the termination date.

7. Ownership of Book of Business

All clients, prospects, policies, expirations, records, and the book of business produced or serviced under this Agreement are and remain the sole and exclusive property of Safeshield. Contractor acknowledges that Contractor holds no ownership interest in the book of business and that the compensation provided under this Agreement is full consideration for the Services.

8. Confidentiality and Proprietary Information

Contractor shall hold in strict confidence all non-public information of the Company, including client data, pricing, carrier relationships, systems, processes, and business strategy, both during and after the term of this Agreement, and shall not use or disclose such information except in the performance of the Services for the Company's benefit.

9. Non-Solicitation and Non-Circumvention

During the term and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following termination, Contractor shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit, divert, write, or service any client or prospect of Safeshield, nor solicit any employee, contractor, or carrier relationship of Safeshield, nor circumvent Safeshield in any business relationship introduced through the Company.

10. Intellectual Property and Work Product

All work product, materials, and intellectual property created by Contractor in connection with the Services are works made for hire and the exclusive property of Safeshield. Contractor assigns to Safeshield all right, title, and interest in such work product.

11. Independent Contractor Status

Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee, partner, or agent of Safeshield for any purpose. Contractor is responsible for Contractor's own taxes and is not entitled to employee benefits. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment or partnership relationship.

12. Indemnification

Contractor shall indemnify and hold Safeshield harmless from any claims, damages, or liabilities arising from Contractor's acts, omissions, or breach of this Agreement.

13. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Connecticut. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Connecticut, and the prevailing Party shall be entitled to recover reasonable attorneys' fees and costs.

14. Survival

Sections 6 through 13 survive termination of this Agreement.

15. Entire Agreement

This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. It may be amended only in a writing signed by both Parties. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force.

By signing below, the Parties agree to the terms of this Agreement.
