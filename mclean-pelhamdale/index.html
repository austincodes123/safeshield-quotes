The most important thing on this page

Your current policy is the wrong type for this property

There are two kinds of policies for a house. A homeowners policy protects a home the owner lives in. A landlord policy protects a home that tenants live in. Your State Farm policy is a homeowners policy, and your own renewal packet lists rental coverage as an option that was never added. Since both apartments at 116 Pelhamdale are rented, a major claim, like a fire or a tenant injury lawsuit, may not be paid because the policy does not match how the property is actually used. The proposal below fixes that, and it costs less per year than what you are paying now.
