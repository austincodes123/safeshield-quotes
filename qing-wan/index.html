Making the Decision
Which Option is Right for You?

Here's the simple way to think about it.

🛡️

Choose Option A if...

You'd rather pay a bit more each year to keep your out-of-pocket costs low if something happens. A $2,500 deductible is much more manageable for most landlords — especially on a rental property where unexpected repairs happen.

💰

Choose Option B if...

You have reserves set aside and don't anticipate frequent small claims. You save $262/yr by taking the higher deductible — and you're still protected against any major or catastrophic loss.

💡 Pro tip: Both options include Replacement Cost coverage — meaning if your property is damaged, American Modern pays to rebuild it at today's prices, not the depreciated value. That's the coverage that really matters.
