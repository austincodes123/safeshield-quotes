Why the Water Backup and Ordinance/Law coverage matter

A 1927 build means older systems and materials throughout the property. Water Backup coverage protects against sewer/drain backup claims, which are common in older Greenwich housing stock, and the Extended Replacement Cost with Ordinance/Law means if the home ever needs to be rebuilt, you're covered up to 25% above the base dwelling limit for the real cost of bringing it up to current code — not just what it looked like before.
